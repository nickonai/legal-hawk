# IFZA (International Free Zone Authority) Compliance Guide

> Reference for SaaS and EdTech companies operating through IFZA, Dubai, UAE.
> Last updated: March 2026. Verify current requirements directly with IFZA before acting.

---

## 1. FZCO Structure and Foreign Ownership

### Entity Type: Free Zone Company (FZCO)

- **Legal form**: Limited liability company established within IFZA (Dubai Silicon Oasis jurisdiction).
- **100% foreign ownership**: Permitted. No local sponsor or UAE national partner required.
- **Shareholders**: Minimum 1, maximum 50. Can be individuals or corporate entities from any nationality.
- **Directors**: Minimum 1 director required. No residency requirement for directors.
- **Share capital**: No minimum share capital requirement at IFZA (unlike some other free zones). However, banks may request evidence of paid-up capital when opening accounts.
- **Legal personality**: FZCO is a separate legal entity. Shareholders' liability is limited to their share capital contribution.
- **Operating scope**: Activities are limited to those specified on the trade license. Operating outside licensed activities is a compliance violation.

### Key Structural Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ownership | 100% foreign permitted |
| Liability | Limited to share capital |
| Minimum shareholders | 1 |
| Maximum shareholders | 50 |
| Local sponsor required | No |
| Physical presence required | Virtual office option available |
| Repatriation of profits | 100% permitted |
| Corporate tax | 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 (Federal CT Law) |

## 2. License Types for SaaS and Education

### Relevant License Categories

**Technology / IT Services License**
- Covers: Software development, SaaS platforms, IT consultancy, cloud services, web/app development.
- Activity codes to consider: "Software Design & Development," "IT Consultancy," "Information Technology Services," "E-Commerce."
- Best fit for: Pure SaaS products, technology platforms, API services.

**Education / Training License**
- Covers: Online training, e-learning platforms, educational content, tutoring services.
- Activity codes to consider: "Educational Support Services," "Training Institute Management," "E-Learning Services."
- Important: If the platform delivers curricula to K-12 students in the UAE, additional approvals from KHDA (Dubai) or MOE (Federal) may be required regardless of free zone licensing.

**Consultancy License**
- Covers: Management consulting, business advisory, professional services.
- Useful for: EdTech companies that provide consulting alongside their platform.

**E-Commerce License**
- Covers: Online sale of goods and services.
- Relevant for: SaaS with direct B2C subscription models or digital product sales.

### Dual / Multiple Activity Licenses

- IFZA permits adding multiple activities to a single license.
- Each additional activity incurs an extra fee (approximately AED 1,500-3,000 per activity).
- Ensure all revenue-generating activities are covered on the license to avoid compliance issues.

### Activity Selection Guidance for SaaS/EdTech

| Business Model | Recommended Activities |
|---|---|
| B2B SaaS platform | Software Design & Development + IT Consultancy |
| B2C EdTech with subscriptions | E-Learning Services + E-Commerce |
| SaaS + consulting | Software Development + Management Consultancy |
| Marketplace / platform | E-Commerce + IT Services |

## 3. Workspace Requirements

### Virtual Office

- **Permitted**: Yes. IFZA allows virtual office packages as the minimum workspace requirement.
- **Includes**: Registered business address, mail handling, use of IFZA address on official documents.
- **Limitations**: Virtual office does not include physical desk space. Meeting rooms can typically be booked on demand.
- **Cost**: Approximately AED 5,000-8,000 per year.

### Flexi Desk

- Shared workspace / hot-desking arrangement.
- Suitable for companies needing occasional physical presence.
- Cost: Approximately AED 8,000-12,000 per year.

### Dedicated Office

- Required if: You employ staff who will physically work from the office, or if your activity requires a physical premises (e.g., training center).
- IFZA offers offices in Dubai Silicon Oasis.
- Cost: Varies by size, starting approximately AED 15,000-25,000 per year.

### Workspace and Visa Relationship

- Virtual office: Typically supports 1-3 visa allocations.
- Flexi desk: Typically supports 3-6 visa allocations.
- Dedicated office: Visa allocation depends on office size (approximately 1 visa per 9 sqm of office space).

## 4. Annual Renewal and Compliance Obligations

### Annual Renewal Requirements

| Obligation | Deadline | Consequence of Non-Compliance |
|---|---|---|
| Trade license renewal | Anniversary of issuance | Late fees; license suspension after 30+ days |
| Lease/workspace renewal | Aligned with license renewal | Cannot renew license without active workspace |
| Immigration card renewal | Aligned with license | Visa cancellation risk |
| Establishment card renewal | Annual | Required for visa processing |

### Ongoing Compliance Obligations

1. **Maintain registered address**: Must have active workspace agreement at all times.
2. **Activity compliance**: Operate only within licensed activities.
3. **Accounting records**: Maintain proper books and financial records (required under UAE Commercial Companies Law and Corporate Tax Law).
4. **Annual audit**: IFZA requires audited financial statements for license renewal.
5. **UBO disclosure**: Ultimate Beneficial Owner information must be filed and kept current.
6. **ESR (Economic Substance Regulations)**: File annual ESR notification. If conducting a "Relevant Activity" (e.g., holding company, IP business, distribution), must demonstrate economic substance in the UAE.
7. **Corporate tax registration**: Register with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) and file annual corporate tax returns.
8. **AML/KYC compliance**: Maintain records per UAE Anti-Money Laundering regulations.
9. **Data protection**: Comply with UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021) -- see separate reference.
10. **Employee records**: Maintain proper employment contracts, visa records, and WPS (Wage Protection System) compliance for any employees.

### Key Renewal Timeline

- **60 days before expiry**: Begin renewal process (license + workspace + immigration card).
- **30 days before expiry**: Deadline to avoid late fees.
- **On expiry**: License becomes inactive. Operating on an expired license is a violation.
- **30+ days after expiry**: Risk of license cancellation and visa revocation.

## 5. Regulated vs. Non-Regulated Activities

### Non-Regulated Activities (Straightforward Licensing)

- Software development
- IT consulting
- General management consulting
- E-commerce (digital goods/services)
- General trading (within free zone)
- Media and marketing services

### Regulated Activities (Require Additional Approvals)

| Activity | Regulatory Body | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Financial services / fintech | DFSA (DIFC) or SCA | Cannot operate from IFZA; requires DIFC or ADGM license |
| Insurance | Insurance Authority | Separate license required |
| Healthcare / healthtech | DHA (Dubai Health Authority) | Approval required for health-related content or services |
| Education (K-12 curricula) | KHDA (Dubai) / MOE (Federal) | Required if delivering formal education to UAE students |
| Food / F&B | Dubai Municipality | Not applicable to SaaS but relevant for food-tech |
| Legal services | MOFA / Courts | Restricted activity |
| Cryptocurrency / virtual assets | VARA (Dubai) | Requires VARA license for crypto-related services |
| Telecommunications | TRA/TDRA | Approval needed for telecom services |
| Media / publishing | National Media Council | May apply to content-heavy platforms |

### SaaS/EdTech Regulatory Considerations

- **Pure SaaS** (B2B software): Generally non-regulated. Standard IFZA license sufficient.
- **EdTech with formal curricula**: If offering accredited courses or K-12 education to UAE residents, KHDA/MOE approval is likely required.
- **EdTech with informal training**: Corporate training, skill development, and professional development platforms generally do not require educational regulatory approval.
- **FinTech elements**: If the SaaS includes payment processing, lending, or investment features, financial services regulations apply.
- **HealthTech**: Health-related advice or data requires DHA/MOH consideration.

## 6. VAT Obligations

### VAT Registration

- **Standard rate**: 5% on taxable supplies.
- **Mandatory registration threshold**: Taxable supplies exceeding AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months (or expected in the next 30 days).
- **Voluntary registration threshold**: Taxable supplies or expenses exceeding AED 187,500.
- **Registration body**: Federal Tax Authority (FTA) via EmaraTax portal.

### VAT Treatment for SaaS

| Scenario | VAT Treatment |
|---|---|
| SaaS sold to UAE customers (B2B or B2C) | 5% VAT applies |
| SaaS sold to GCC customers (non-VAT registered) | 5% VAT applies |
| SaaS sold to GCC customers (VAT registered, in implementing state) | Reverse charge (0% from supplier) |
| SaaS sold to customers outside GCC | Zero-rated (0%) -- export of services |
| Free zone to free zone (designated zone) | May be zero-rated if conditions met |

### Key VAT Compliance Steps

1. Register for VAT with FTA once threshold is met.
2. Obtain Tax Registration Number (TRN).
3. Issue VAT-compliant tax invoices (must include TRN, VAT amount, etc.).
4. File VAT returns quarterly (or as determined by FTA).
5. Maintain records for 5 years.
6. Correctly apply place-of-supply rules for digital services.

### Place of Supply for Digital Services

- B2B: Place of supply is where the recipient belongs (reverse charge may apply).
- B2C: Place of supply is where the supplier belongs (UAE), so 5% VAT applies for UAE-based supplier selling to consumers worldwide unless the customer is outside the GCC.
- Export of services (outside GCC): Zero-rated if conditions are met (evidence of customer location, no performance in UAE).

## 7. Bank Account Requirements

### Corporate Bank Account

- **Required**: Not legally mandatory to operate, but practically essential for receiving payments, paying salaries (WPS), and tax compliance.
- **Timing**: Apply after license issuance. Process takes 2-8 weeks depending on the bank.
- **Challenges**: UAE banks have strict KYC/AML requirements. New free zone companies, especially those without physical substance or local revenue, may face rejections.

### Documentation Typically Required

- Trade license copy
- Certificate of incorporation / good standing
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Shareholders' passport copies and UAE visa copies (if applicable)
- Proof of address for shareholders (home country utility bill, bank statement)
- Business plan / company profile
- Expected transaction volumes and sources of funds
- UBO declaration
- Board resolution to open the account

### Bank Account Tips

1. **Apply to multiple banks simultaneously** (at least 3-4).
2. **Consider digital/challenger banks**: Mashreq NEO, Wio Bank, and similar offer faster onboarding for free zone companies.
3. **Maintain substance**: Banks look favorably on UAE-based employees, local clients, and office presence.
4. **Initial deposit**: Some banks require AED 10,000-50,000 minimum opening balance.
5. **Multi-currency accounts**: Important for SaaS companies receiving international payments (USD, EUR, GBP).
6. **Payment gateway integration**: Ensure the bank supports integration with your payment processor (Stripe, PayTabs, etc.).

## 8. Visa Allocation

### Visa Entitlements

- IFZA FZCO can sponsor employment and investor visas for shareholders, employees, and their dependents.
- **Visa duration**: Typically 2-3 years, renewable.
- **Investor visa**: Available for shareholders/directors.
- **Employment visa**: For staff members.
- **Dependent visas**: Spouse and children can be sponsored if the visa holder meets minimum salary thresholds (currently AED 4,000/month or AED 3,000/month + accommodation).

### Visa Allocation by Workspace

| Workspace Type | Typical Visa Allocation |
|---|---|
| Virtual office | 1-3 visas |
| Flexi desk | 3-6 visas |
| Dedicated office | Based on sqm (approx. 1 per 9 sqm) |

### Visa Process Steps

1. Apply for entry permit (if applicant is outside UAE) or status change (if inside UAE on visit visa).
2. Medical fitness test.
3. Emirates ID registration.
4. Visa stamping.
5. Labor card issuance (for employees).

### Important Visa Notes

- **Remote employees outside UAE**: Do not need UAE visas. Contractor agreements can be used.
- **Freelance / part-time**: IFZA does not issue freelance permits. Consider DAFZA or other free zones for freelancers.
- **Golden Visa**: Available for investors (AED 2M+ investment) or specialized talent. Not issued through IFZA directly but IFZA license can support the application.

## Practical Checklist for SaaS/EdTech IFZA Setup

- [ ] Determine correct activity codes for your business model
- [ ] Verify whether any activities are regulated (KHDA, VARA, DHA, etc.)
- [ ] Choose workspace type (virtual office is sufficient for most SaaS)
- [ ] Prepare incorporation documents (passport, proof of address, business plan)
- [ ] Submit IFZA application and obtain trade license
- [ ] Apply for visa(s) if needed
- [ ] Open corporate bank account (apply to multiple banks)
- [ ] Register for VAT with FTA (if threshold met or voluntary)
- [ ] Register for Corporate Tax with FTA
- [ ] Engage an auditor for annual financial statements
- [ ] Set up accounting / bookkeeping system
- [ ] File annual ESR notification
- [ ] Implement data protection measures per UAE PDPL
- [ ] Set calendar reminders for all renewal deadlines (60 days in advance)
