# UAE Website Compliance Audit Checklist

> 40+ item checklist for SaaS and EdTech websites operating from IFZA / UAE.
> Use this for pre-launch audits and periodic compliance reviews.
> Last updated: March 2026.

---

## How to Use

- Review each item and check the box when compliant.
- Items marked **(Critical)** are legally required and expose the business to penalties if missing.
- Items marked **(Recommended)** are best practice and expected by regulators, investors, and enterprise customers.
- Review this checklist quarterly or whenever significant changes are made to the website, product, or legal structure.

---

## A. Company and IFZA Information

- [ ] **(Critical)** Full legal company name displayed (as registered with IFZA), including "FZCO" suffix
- [ ] **(Critical)** IFZA trade license number displayed
- [ ] **(Critical)** Registered business address (IFZA office / virtual office address)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Contact email address (functional, monitored inbox)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Contact phone number
- [ ] **(Recommended)** TRN (Tax Registration Number) displayed if VAT registered
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Free zone identified: "Registered in IFZA, Dubai, UAE" or equivalent
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Company information accessible from every page (footer is standard placement)

---

## B. Legal Documents

### Privacy Policy

- [ ] **(Critical)** Privacy policy published and accessible via link from every page (typically footer)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Privacy policy identifies the data controller (company name, address, contact)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Purposes of data processing clearly stated
- [ ] **(Critical)** Lawful bases for processing identified for each purpose
- [ ] **(Critical)** Categories of personal data collected are listed
- [ ] **(Critical)** Third-party recipients / processors disclosed
- [ ] **(Critical)** Cross-border data transfers disclosed with safeguards described
- [ ] **(Critical)** Data retention periods stated
- [ ] **(Critical)** Data subject rights listed (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction, withdraw consent)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Contact method for exercising data subject rights provided (email or form)
- [ ] **(Critical)** DPO contact information provided (if DPO appointed)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Privacy policy available in Arabic
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Date of last update shown on the privacy policy
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Children's data handling described (if platform may be used by minors)

### Terms of Service / Terms of Use

- [ ] **(Critical)** Terms of Service published and accessible
- [ ] **(Critical)** Click-wrap acceptance mechanism at sign-up ("I agree to the Terms of Service" checkbox)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Governing law specified (UAE Federal Law)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Jurisdiction / dispute resolution specified
- [ ] **(Critical)** Limitation of liability clause included
- [ ] **(Critical)** Intellectual property ownership and license terms stated
- [ ] **(Critical)** Acceptable use policy defined (prohibited activities)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Termination and suspension conditions stated
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Service description and scope defined
- [ ] **(Recommended)** SLA (uptime, support response times) referenced or linked
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Force majeure clause included
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Version date and change notification process stated
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Terms available in Arabic

### Cookie Policy

- [ ] **(Critical)** Cookie consent banner displayed on first visit
- [ ] **(Critical)** Banner provides option to accept or reject non-essential cookies
- [ ] **(Critical)** Non-essential cookies not loaded before consent is given
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Granular cookie categories available (necessary, analytics, marketing, functional)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Cookie policy page listing all cookies, their purpose, duration, and provider
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Mechanism to withdraw cookie consent (accessible settings link)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Cookie preferences are remembered and respected on return visits

## C. PDPL (Data Protection) Compliance

- [ ] **(Critical)** Consent collected before processing personal data where consent is the lawful basis
- [ ] **(Critical)** Consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous (not pre-checked boxes)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Mechanism for users to withdraw consent (as easy as giving consent)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Data subject rights request process is functional (form, email, or in-app)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Identity verification process for data subject requests is in place
- [ ] **(Critical)** Data processing agreements executed with all third-party processors
- [ ] **(Critical)** Record of processing activities (ROPA) maintained internally
- [ ] **(Critical)** Data breach notification procedure documented and tested
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Data protection impact assessment conducted for high-risk processing
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Data minimization principle applied (only collect what is necessary)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Retention schedules defined and automated deletion in place
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Staff trained on data protection obligations

---

## D. E-Commerce Compliance

### Pricing and Payment

- [ ] **(Critical)** Prices displayed in AED (UAE Dirhams) -- may show other currencies alongside
- [ ] **(Critical)** VAT treatment clear: prices shown inclusive of VAT, or "plus 5% VAT" clearly indicated
- [ ] **(Critical)** Total cost displayed before payment confirmation (no hidden fees)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Subscription pricing clearly stated: amount, billing frequency, auto-renewal terms
- [ ] **(Critical)** Free trial terms clearly stated: duration, conversion to paid, payment method collection
- [ ] **(Critical)** Accepted payment methods listed
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Currency converter or AED equivalent shown if pricing is in foreign currency
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Payment page uses HTTPS with valid SSL/TLS certificate
- [ ] **(Recommended)** PCI DSS compliance badge or statement displayed on payment pages

### Refund and Cancellation

- [ ] **(Critical)** Refund policy published and accessible before purchase
- [ ] **(Critical)** Cancellation process clearly described (steps, where to click, who to contact)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Cancellation effective date stated (immediate, end of billing period, etc.)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Self-service cancellation available (not requiring contacting support only)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Refund timeline stated (e.g., "refunds processed within 14 business days")
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Pro-rata refund policy stated for annual subscriptions cancelled early

### Transaction Process

- [ ] **(Critical)** Order confirmation sent via email after purchase
- [ ] **(Critical)** VAT-compliant invoice provided (includes TRN, VAT amount, company details)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Transaction records stored for minimum 5 years
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Invoice accessible from user account dashboard
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Payment failure notification and retry mechanism

---

## E. Advertising and Marketing

- [ ] **(Critical)** All advertising claims are truthful, accurate, and substantiated
- [ ] **(Critical)** No misleading pricing or bait-and-switch tactics
- [ ] **(Critical)** Marketing emails sent only to users who have opted in
- [ ] **(Critical)** Unsubscribe mechanism present and functional in all marketing emails
- [ ] **(Critical)** Sponsored content and paid partnerships clearly labeled
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Testimonials and reviews are genuine
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Comparative claims about competitors are objective and verifiable
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Marketing consent is separate from Terms of Service acceptance (not bundled)

---

## F. Education-Specific (KHDA / MOE)

> Applicable if the platform delivers educational content to K-12 students or offers accredited programs in the UAE.

- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** KHDA approval obtained for education services delivered to students in Dubai
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** MOE approval obtained for education services with national curriculum alignment
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** Parental consent mechanism for students under 18
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** Age verification or age gate at registration
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** No behavioral advertising or profiling of minor users
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** Child-friendly privacy notice published
- [ ] **(Critical -- if applicable)** Parental access to child's account data and deletion capability
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Content accuracy and quality standards documented
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Teacher/instructor qualifications verified and displayed (if applicable)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Student data stored separately with enhanced security controls
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Data retention for student records aligned with educational requirements and PDPL

## G. Technical Requirements

### Security

- [ ] **(Critical)** HTTPS enabled across entire website (valid SSL/TLS certificate)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Payment processing via PCI DSS compliant provider
- [ ] **(Critical)** User passwords stored using strong hashing (bcrypt, argon2, or equivalent)
- [ ] **(Critical)** Protection against common vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10: XSS, CSRF, SQL injection, etc.)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Two-factor authentication (2FA) available for user accounts
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Security headers implemented (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, etc.)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Regular security testing (penetration testing, vulnerability scanning)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Incident response plan documented

### Accessibility

- [ ] **(Recommended)** Website meets WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Alt text on images
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Keyboard navigable
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Screen reader compatible
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Sufficient color contrast ratios

### Performance and Availability

- [ ] **(Recommended)** Website loads within 3 seconds on standard connections
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Mobile-responsive design
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Uptime monitoring in place
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Error pages (404, 500) are branded and helpful

### Domain and Hosting

- [ ] **(Recommended)** Domain registration details kept current (WHOIS)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Data hosting location documented (relevant for PDPL cross-border transfers)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Backup and disaster recovery procedures documented
- [ ] **(Recommended)** CDN configured for UAE region performance (if serving UAE customers)

---

## H. Contractual and Business Documents

> Not necessarily website-published, but must exist and be referenced or accessible.

- [ ] **(Critical)** Data Processing Agreements with all third-party processors
- [ ] **(Critical)** Subprocessor list maintained (and disclosed in privacy policy or on request)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** SLA document available for enterprise customers
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Acceptable Use Policy published
- [ ] **(Recommended)** DMCA / copyright takedown process documented
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Bug bounty or responsible disclosure policy (for security researchers)
- [ ] **(Recommended)** Status page for service availability (e.g., status.yourcompany.com)

## Priority Actions After Audit

1. Address all **(Critical)** non-compliant items immediately.
2. Create a timeline for **(Recommended)** items (target: within 30 days).
3. Mark **(N/A)** items with justification (e.g., education items not applicable if no minors).
4. Document remediation actions and responsible parties.
5. Schedule next audit (quarterly recommended, minimum annually).
